  ** HOW TO READ THE DELTA COLUMNS. They are NOT a measure of how much
     information a component carries. Leave-one-out with renormalised
     weights redistributes the dropped weight to the remaining terms,
     so a component whose VALUE is far below the others raises the
     score when removed. That is why dS is strongly POSITIVE: S
     averages about 0.01 while (1-H), (1-A) and (1-G) average near 1.
     The delta measures a component's value relative to the rest. **
